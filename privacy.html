Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026

The short version: we don't collect anything. Not your name, not your times, not even a cookie. Everything stays on your device.

This policy covers BrassCount, our shot timer app for Android, and this website, rubberduckco.co.za. Both are made by Rubber Duck Co.

What we collect

Nothing. BrassCount does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no servers that receive data from it.

Microphone

BrassCount asks for permission to use your phone's microphone. It uses the microphone for two things:

  • detecting the sound of your shots while the timer is running
  • calibration, where it measures how loud your shots are so it can tell them apart from other noise

The sound is processed on your phone as it happens. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

You can turn off microphone access at any time in your phone's settings. Without it, BrassCount can't hear your shots.

Data on your phone

BrassCount saves your drills, calibration settings and log, such as shot times and splits, on your phone so you can use them again and look back on them. This data stays on your phone. We can't see it, and we can't recover it for you.

You can delete it at any time by clearing BrassCount's data in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Third parties

BrassCount doesn't include any third-party services, such as advertising, analytics or crash reporting tools, so nothing is shared with anyone. Google Play handles downloads and updates under Google's privacy policy.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking, and doesn't load anything from other services. The brass counter at the top of the page is saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only, so your pile is still there when you come back. Sweeping it up or clearing your browser data removes it.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure its service. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Children

We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. If BrassCount ever needs to collect any data, we'll say so here before that version is released.
