BrassCount

A shot timer for your Android phone. Set up a drill, wait for the beep, shoot. BrassCount hears every shot and shows your first shot, your splits and your total time.

Coming soon to Google Play

Timer
A random start delay, a loud beep, and every shot picked up through your phone's microphone.
Drills
Build your own drills with par times, start delays and shot counts, then run them as often as you like.
Calibration
Tune shot detection to your gun and your range, indoors or out, so it counts your shots and not the bay next door.
Log
Every string, split and session is saved on your phone, so you can see whether you're getting faster.

Your data stays on your phone.

BrassCount has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your drills, times and calibration are saved on your phone and nowhere else. We never see any of it.
